Legal MVP path

Vercel app first. Regulated money through partners.

  1. Build the marketplace, profiles, dashboards, messaging, updates, and admin review.
  2. Use Stripe only for approved non-investment services like subscriptions, bookings, or a la carte offers.
  3. Route investment money, escrow, investor checks, closings, and securities records through a registered funding portal or broker-dealer.
  4. Store partner events read-only, then reconcile holdings, distributions, and reporting inside HFC.
