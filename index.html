Representation model

The person is the investment. Stock is the receipt.

Trust agreement

Investor and investee agree to work toward aligned goals with transparent reporting and shared upside.

C-corp wrapper

The company exists to give the agreement books, taxes, ownership records, distributions, and enforceable obligations.

Stock today

Non-voting securities are the cleanest available representation because law already understands private company ownership.

Tokens later

Crypto could represent fixed-price private stock if valuation changes only through approved events, not market speculation.

Accountability layer

Open enough to trust. Flexible enough to live.

Revenue tracking

Sync bank, accounting, and service-payment data into included, excluded, or review-needed revenue events.

Investor updates

Post milestones, spend, progress, asks, polls, and risks so backers know what is happening.

Clear exclusions

Separate company revenue from salary, unrelated startups, grants, and personal activity before payouts are calculated.

Aligned help

Investors can offer intros, hiring help, sales leads, feedback, accounting help, or practical resources.

Legal MVP path

Vercel app first. Regulated money through partners.

  1. Build the marketplace, profiles, dashboards, messaging, updates, and admin review.
  2. Use Stripe only for approved non-investment services like subscriptions, bookings, or a la carte offers.
  3. Route investment money, escrow, investor checks, closings, and securities records through a registered funding portal or broker-dealer.
  4. Store partner events in an append-only ledger so stock records now can become tokenized private securities later.
